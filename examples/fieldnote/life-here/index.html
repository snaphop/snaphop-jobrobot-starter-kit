Life here

A week at Riverside Care

No two weeks are identical, but a few things are always true.

How the week runs

  • Monday huddles

    Fifteen minutes to plan the week together, so nobody finds out about a change on Thursday.

  • Protected admin time

    Notes and follow-ups are part of the schedule, not something you do after hours.

  • Case reviews

    A fortnightly space to talk through difficult cases with peers and a senior clinician.

  • Friday finish

    Clinics close early on Fridays. Emergency cover rotates fairly across the team.

What you can count on

  • Pay and leave

    Transparent salary bands, paid leave from day one, and paid time for licence renewals.

  • Health cover

    Medical, dental and vision plans for you and your dependants.

  • Learning

    An annual training budget and supervision toward your next qualification.
